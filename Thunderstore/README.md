# Tweyen, Eternal of the Grand Blue Skies

[![GitHub Suggestions / Issues](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/nepsionion/TweyenR2RMod/main/Art/Readme/github_issues.png)](https://github.com/nepsionion/TweyenR2RMod/issues)

Tweyen, or Song (see config), is the second Eternal and master of the bow from Granblue Fantasy. She is a ranged hunter who has two gameplay styles, one for each of her passives.

## Description

Tweyen is faster than other survivors, and her arrows home in on nearby targets. Stay on the move to dodge enemy fire while your arrows wear enemies down.

Her first passive, **Twin-Star Huntress**, leans into debuffs. Your attacks apply random debuffs that will not only cover for her lower-than-average DPS, but also empower **Clincher**, her Secondary skill. Debuffs from items also enhance this skill, so items like *Tri-Tip Dagger* and *Chronobauble* synergize especially well with her kit.
These debuffs can also be extended and intensified with her Special, **Two-Crown's Strife**. However, Two-Crown's Strife is a once-per-stage skill (configurable), so hold it for the boss or the swarm that would otherwise overwhelm you, and make it count.

Her other passive, **Peerless Eye**, accumulates a buff that gives increasingly greater attack speed, and at enough stacks, guaranteed critical strikes on Tweyen's Secondary and Special skills. However, you lose your ability to consistently inflict debuffs, so choose carefully.

## Features

- A full character with Relink's models, animations, effects, sounds and voice lines.
- 11 character skins and 4 bow skins (some earned through new achievements)
- Two distinct Passive skills
- Alternative Secondary and Utility skills
- Multiplayer compatible (probably).
- Config Options:
  - first-person aiming for **Asteroid Jaeger** (Secondary skill)
  - disable voice lines (only Japanese voice lines are implemented)
  - disable movement sound
  - reusable Two-Crown's Strife (Special skill)
  - change her name to Song (her Japanese name)
  - unlock the skins that require DLC
- Ancient Scepter support, with an upgraded Special if [StandaloneAncientScepter](https://thunderstore.io/c/riskofrain2/p/amogus_lovers/StandaloneAncientScepter/) is installed (optional).
